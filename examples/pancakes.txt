# Fluffy pancakes
Serves 4

2 cups all-purpose flour
2 tbsp sugar
2 tsp baking powder
½ tsp salt
1½ cups milk
2 eggs
3 tbsp butter, melted
a pinch of nutmeg

1. Whisk the dry ingredients.
2. Add the milk, eggs and butter and stir until just combined.
3. Cook on a hot pan, about ¼ cup of batter per pancake.
